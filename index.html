About

Teacher in the classroom. Engineer in industry.

I teach robotics, engineering and programming, and I work as an industrial and systems engineer too. I've improved how work gets done on projects for companies like TracFone Wireless, Volkswagen and Vesta-z, and I bring the same design, test and improve cycle into both.

I'm open to roles in EdTech and engineering education where I can bring teaching, process improvement and business analysis together, and help schools and EdTech teams put AI to work in ways that save time and remove bottlenecks.

Teaching

My students learn engineering by working like engineers.

I've taught robotics, engineering and programming for more than ten years: in classrooms in Florida and France, and live online to K–12 students through Varsity Tutors and Playful Circuits, the online teaching service I founded. I'm also a Systems Improvement Engineer: I still consult on engineering projects while I teach, and I bring that way of working into the lab.

My students define the problem, find out who they're solving it for, then build, test, listen to feedback and adjust. When a robot doesn't work, I don't fix it. I ask them to start with the basics and find the problem themselves. Most failures are small, and finding them teaches students that a problem can be solved.

Engineering Design Process

Every project starts with two questions: who has this problem, and who are we solving it for? Then students build, test and improve, the way a design team works.

Telecom · 2007–2010

TracFone Wireless

Systems Design QA & UAT Customer Retention

Led the design and launch of the online My Account capabilities for a national phone carrier.

TracFone Wireless (América Móvil) was losing customers for a reason that had nothing to do with price: people couldn't manage their own accounts, so they called support for everything. As Systems Improvement Engineer, I led the design and launch of the online My Account capabilities, turning key customer-service journeys into digital self-service for account management, airtime replenishment, device management and service tracking. Industrial Parts · B2B Software · 2024–Present

Vesta-z

Process Automation AI-Assisted Workflow Sales Operations

Replaced manual reading of customer PDF requests with automated extraction.

Vesta-z sells machinery parts procurement to European industrial buyers. Staff were reading each customer's PDF request by hand, separating out the machine parts and answering questions one at a time. Through Hip Dot Media, I mapped that process and worked with the technical team to replace it with automated extraction that fills in the results. I also rebuilt the commercial process around structured outreach and data-based lead qualification.

Outcome: Turnaround on customer requests went from days to about an hour.

EdTech · 2020–2023

Playful Circuits

Founder Program Design Service Delivery

Designed how a live online robotics school would actually run, from class size to tool stack.

Playful Circuits (playfulcircuits.com) is my own company. I designed the operating model: classes of six to ten students so instructors could still give attention to each kid, a Zoom-based delivery flow, and a tool stack built on CodeSpark, LEGO Education, and Scratch. The point was that an instructor could pick it up and run it without inventing the class again every week.

Outcome: Documented the delivery system so a second instructor could teach classes to the same standard.

Retail / eCommerce · Founded 2011 · Repositioned 2022

Fetes de Lili

Founder Market Repositioning Digital Operations

Rebuilt my own eco-friendly party supply business around a customer who had completely changed.

This one is mine. I founded it in 2011, and from 2012 to 2016 I tested an eco-friendly party supply line through Zulily and Groupon. After COVID the market moved underneath us. The customers were different, mostly older parents with children up to about ten, and they were buying online in a way they hadn't before. The old site didn't match how any of them were shopping. So I started with the research, figured out who was actually buying, and rebuilt the digital side around them instead of around who we used to sell to.

Outcome: A storefront built around the people who were actually buying, instead of the ones who used to.
